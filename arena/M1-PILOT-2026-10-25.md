## Why the batch stopped

Codex emitted four top-level reconnection errors and an `item.completed` error
announcing fallback from WebSockets to HTTPS. The WebSocket errors report a peer
closing the TLS connection without `close_notify`. It subsequently completed the
useful task, emitted usage and exited successfully.

The frozen adapter records those error events as `provider_error` /
`provider_failure`, and does not recognize the error item (`unknown_item:7:error`).
The controller therefore stopped with `evidence_or_cleanup_incomplete`.
**Cleanup itself was verified**; the reason was the stream's evidence gaps.
The trace establishes successful HTTPS fallback, but does not by itself identify
which component caused the WebSocket closure. This is a transport/adapter
integration finding, not evidence of a containment bypass or a failed slug fix.

Codex's client made its own internal reconnection attempts within slot 2. These
are preserved; they are distinct from starting another Arena attempt. No
automatic Arena retry occurred, and no original score was edited to continue.
Offline replay of both raw bundles produced scores identical to the originals.

Claude reported exactly `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929`. Codex was configured for
`gpt-6-astra` with `xhigh`, but its stream did not report an actual model ID.
Requested and reported model identity remain separate claims.
